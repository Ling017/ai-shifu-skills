# Decision gates

Before finalizing check:
- Topic, audience, advantage, and promise trace to evidence or explicit hypotheses.
- Teaching sufficiency fits objective and format, not lesson count.
- Market claims have relevant dated evidence; blocked research is unverified.
- Need, payment intent, public signals, sales, and learning proof remain distinct.
- Explicit market, research restrictions, confidentiality, and scope are preserved.
- No invented primary, backups, competitors, risks, scores, or price to fill a format.

## Status mapping
- recommended / GO: adequate material, delivery fit, and relevant market evidence justify the stated next step, not guaranteed sales or launch authorization.
- pending-validation / HOLD: plausible but key source, market, or payment evidence is missing. Material-only work cannot establish commercial GO.
- pending-validation / REWORK: material has value but scope, audience, format, or promise needs revision.
- not-recommended / NO-GO: evidence supports rejecting the current proposal. Explain whether this rejects course form, audience, or the entire direction. Missing evidence alone is not NO-GO.

State actual risks and decision impact without a fixed risk count. Scores are optional aids with reasoning, never substitutes for evidence gates.
